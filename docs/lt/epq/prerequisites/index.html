Prerequisites

Two routes

Exercises or example projects either require use of Orange (and no coding skills) or Python (with coding skills).

Orange Data Mining is a visual tool in which you build a workflow by connecting widgets. No coding skills are required.

Python is a general purpose programming language, widely used for AI. Some projects or exercises need an Apple silicon Mac (M-series) with 16 GB of memory.

If your school provides the computers, ask first to make sure you have sufficient access and are able to install the software.

Software

Route You install
Orange Orange Data Mining and, for some outlines, the orange3-ai-suite add-on with one of its extras (image, lora)
Python Python 3.11 or later, and the packages the outline names: mlx-lm, numpy, matplotlib, psutil

Everything runs locally once installed. Nothing here needs an account, a subscription, or a cloud service.

Downloads

Be mindful that some projects may involve large downloads.

  • Language models are the largest item. BritLLM is about 5 GB in full and 1–2 GB per quantised version, and a project that compares quantisations holds several at once.
  • Image corpora run to hundreds of megabytes; the WMO International Cloud Atlas is the example in this pack.
  • Tabular data is small: Gapminder indicators are a few megabytes, and the World Bank is a useful cross-check.

Datasets such as MNIST ship as a prepared subset or in full.

Check the licence of anything you download if you intend to redistribute what you produce.

Permission and ethics

Some projects bring you into contact with people or with other people’s material:

  • Asking classmates for writing, answers or judgements may involve consent, anonymity, a right to withdraw, and storage your school needs to accept.
  • Photographing a site may mean being off campus, on site at a particular time, or both.
  • Using images or corpora you did not create may have licences restricting use or requiring a citation.

Remember to discuss with your supervisor what your school requires, and when it needs it.
