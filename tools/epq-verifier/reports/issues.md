# Potential issues in the EPQ project outlines

Generated by `tools/epq-verifier/run.py`. This is not a verdict: it is a list of
places where an outline could be read as endorsing a practice that would cost a
student marks, or that a moderator would query.

- 6 outlines checked against 27 rules (13 judged, 14 text).
- 5 potential issues (0 high severity).
- Rules that are about the pack as a whole also read its prerequisites page.
- Judge: `deepseek-v4.1-flash`, reasoning `low`, 2 run(s) per outline, only findings in all of them reported.

## Across the whole pack

### CLAIM-01 — Says what usually happens, without a source

*med · tone* · prerequisites

**Why:** We have not surveyed students, projects or schools, so a claim about what is common, usual or typical is a guess wearing the clothes of evidence - and a student can be misled by it.

**Suggested:** State the fact or the risk without claiming it is common.

<sub>https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/subjects/AQA-W-7993-SP-19.PDF — §1.2 What is the Extended Project?: “It will involve extended autonomous work by the student.”</sub>

### PACK-BOARD — The pack never points the student at their board's requirements

*med · pack* · whole pack

**Why:** The pack is not a substitute for the specification: the log, the report, the presentation and the word lengths are the board's to set, and they differ between boards.

**Suggested:** Tell the student, once, to check their board's specification with their supervisor.

<sub>https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/subjects/AQA-W-7993-SP-19.PDF — §1.2 What is the Extended Project?: “choose an area of interest”</sub>

### TONE-03 — Says which part of the project matters most

*med · tone* · projects

**Why:** The pack does not set the weighting. 'An important part of the project' is the pack rating its own sections, which is how a page starts promising marks for a method and apologising for a result.

**Suggested:** Describe the section and what it holds; leave the weight to the assessment.

<sub>https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/subjects/AQA-W-7993-SP-19.PDF — Appendix A Grade Descriptions: “The award will depend in practice upon the extent to which the candidate has met the Assessment Objectives (see Section 2.4.3) overall.”</sub>

### TONE-04 — Instructs the student, rather than inviting them to consider

*low · tone* · prerequisites

**Why:** A page that remembers things on the student's behalf - 'remember to', 'make sure', 'don't forget' - is managing the project, and takes on the responsibility for whether it happened.

**Suggested:** Put the thing in front of the student and let them decide: 'consider whether...' rather than 'remember to...'.

<sub>https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/subjects/AQA-W-7993-SP-19.PDF — §2.3 What is the Project process?: “carry out the project by achieving the aims and objectives they set themselves”</sub>
